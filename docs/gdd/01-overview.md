# 01 Overview — Decided
> Read when: need the big picture or design pillars.

- **OVR-1** Genre: 2D hero-collecting (gacha) RPG.
- **OVR-2** Combat: same mechanics as *Assassin's Creed Rebellion* → `02-combat.md`.
- **OVR-3** Setting is **not** Assassin's Creed. Setting undecided (Q-01). All names are placeholders.
- **OVR-4** Modes: Campaign, Dungeons, Events; more later (Q-17).
- **OVR-5** Champions come mainly from summoning; some are rewards for missions (e.g. 3-star every campaign stage).

## Pillars (use these to settle small calls without asking)
1. **Simple champion building** — easy to understand at a glance.
2. **No RNG in progression** — fixed gear stats, no failure chances, no random skill-ups. (Summoning is the only random system.)
3. **Tactics beat raw power** — smart team choice lets players punch above their level.
4. **Nothing is wasted** — duplicates convert, dismantling refunds.
5. **Every rarity/star is useful** — upgrades are bonuses, not requirements.

## Business & platform — Decided (D-12)
- **OVR-6** Business model: free-to-play with IAP, ads and battle pass. First public build is a **beta without purchases**.
- **OVR-7** Platform: Android first, Google Play. **Landscape only.**
- **OVR-8** Multiple languages (list: Q-21). No Persian.
- **OVR-9** Launch scope (Volumes, roster size, modes) is decided after the demo (Q-20).
