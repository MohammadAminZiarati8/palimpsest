# C02 Pell — starter Assassin, Decided for demo (D-46, all per Claude's recommendation), may change after
> Unwritten · Assassin · Starter 2, joins in the Prologue (D-38). Rarity **Rare**.

## Concept — "The Half-Erased"
- Young man, calm, quiet, dry humour. Margo's opposite: she rushes in, he sighs and follows.
- The Redactor's Censors nearly erased him; parts of him are faint like rubbed-out pencil. He knows what erasure feels like.
- Name from *palimpsest* / *pellis* (parchment scraped and rewritten). Trademark check before launch.

## Look
- Dark grey hooded cloak whose edges fade into pencil-sketch lines.
- Left arm partly see-through, drawn in faint grey strokes.
- Silver-grey eyes. Accent colour: cool teal (contrasts Margo's orange-red).
- Weapon: twin daggers shaped like letter-openers (light blades, CLS-7).
