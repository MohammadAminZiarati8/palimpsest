# C01 Margo — main hero, Decided for demo (D-39..45), may change after
> Unwritten · Warrior · Starter 1, joins in the Prologue (D-38). Skills/stats: below, Proposed when added.

## Concept — "The Eager Blade"
- **Young woman** (D-40). Cheerful, brave, a bit reckless.
- Born in the Margin with no story; wants to earn a story worth telling.
- Weapon: huge greatsword shaped like a pen nib (heavy melee, CLS-7).
- With Folio: Folio teases them constantly; comic duo.
- Fits light → dark (STY-2): starts bright, grows tougher as the world darkens.

## Look — "Blank-Page Adventurer" (D-42)
- Light travel clothes in cream/parchment, boots, fingerless gloves.
- Short messy ink-black hair with one white streak.
- **Long blank paper scarf** = her missing story; words appear on it with each awakening star (AWK flavor: a restored chapter).
- Accent colour: warm orange-red (reads on green Seedbed and dark realms).
