## Concept — "The Healer of Hangzhou"
- A white snake spirit who studied magic for centuries, took human form and ran a medicine shop that healed her town.
- Calm, warm, gently teasing; fierce when friends are threatened. Older-sister figure to Margo, Pell and Libby.
- With Folio: polite on the surface, quietly wins every argument.
- Teases a future Chinese-legends realm (not yet planned).

## Respect & IP (UNI IP rules)

## Look
- Long black hair with a white jade hairpin.
- Flowing white robes (Song-era inspired) with faint silver scale patterns.
- Her shadow on the ground is a great coiled serpent.
- Accent colour: jade green.
- Weapon: white willow staff hung with small medicine pouches (focus, CLS-7).
