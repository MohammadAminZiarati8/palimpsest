# C03 Libby — Seedbed Mage, Decided for demo (D-47, all per Claude's recommendation), may change after
> Unwritten · Mage · rescued at the end of the Seedbed (D-25, D-38). Free story champion (STY-6). Rarity **Rare**.

## Concept — "The Sprout"
- Young girl, the youngest of the team. Gentle, shy, speaks little, braver than she looks.
- Grew up in the Seedbed among the page-trees; carries its magic with her after the rescue.
- Margo adopts her as a little sister; Pell is quietly protective.
- Name from Latin *liber*: both "inner tree bark" and "book". Trademark check before launch.

## Look
- Small; moss-green hair with tiny paper leaves growing in it.
- Oversized tunic made of layered leaf-pages; root-like wraps on arms and feet.
- Accent colour: soft sunlight yellow (stands out on the green Seedbed).
- Weapon: a scroll that sprouts leaves and vines (Arcana, CLS-7).
