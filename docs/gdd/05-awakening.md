# 05 Awakening — Decided (Simple Stars, inspired by *Watcher of Realms*)
> Read when: duplicates, Soul Stones, stars.

- **AWK-1** A duplicate champion becomes a Soul Stone for that specific champion.
- **AWK-2** Soul Stones go to inventory; nothing automatic. Player chooses when/if to use.
- **AWK-3** 1 Soul Stone = 1 star. Max 5 stars.
- **AWK-4** Every star gives + stats.
- **AWK-5** ⭐3: one skill becomes Awakened (bonus effect, e.g. hits 2 enemies).
- **AWK-6** ⭐5: one more skill becomes Awakened (no Ultimates exist, CHM-15). ⭐3 → S2, ⭐5 → S3 (CMB-28).
- **AWK-7** Universal Soul Stone: rare (chapter rewards, events); works on any champion of the matching rarity.
- **AWK-8** After ⭐5, extra Soul Stones exchange for a shop currency. Duplicates never wasted.
- **AWK-9** Awakening is a bonus, not required: a 0-star champion must be useful.
