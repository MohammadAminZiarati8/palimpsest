# 00 Universe — Decided (approved 2026-10-16, D-09)
> Read when: writing story, lore, champions, enemies, stage themes. All names: trademark check before launch.

## Frame — Decided (D-07)
- **UNI-1** Original world we own (lore, villain, factions, original heroes).
- **UNI-2** Public-domain legends live in it as champions (myth, folklore, pre-1930 literature, long-dead historical figures).
- **UNI-3** Content is organised into realms; a campaign chapter group = one realm.

## Premise
- **World: Palimpsest** — a world made of every story ever told, each realm a *Volume*. Written over again and again, never finished.
- **Villain: The Redactor** — once the world's Archivist. He lost someone to a tragic ending and decided stories must be controlled: one True Text, every ending his. He *redacts* legends — erasing their will and rewriting them as obedient **Revised**. Grey motive, not pure evil.
- **His forces:** **Censors** (faceless ink-soldiers, the common enemy) · realm tyrants who joined him willingly (e.g. Zahhak). Bosses are never recruitable (D-14).
- **Revised** legends are his captives, held ink-bound in his strongholds. They are never fought: the Scribe reaches and frees them, and a freed legend joins as a champion (D-14).
- **Rebels: The Unbound** — legends who escaped redaction. Hidden HQ: **the Margin**, the blank space between Volumes.
- **Player: the Scribe** — holder of the **Last Quill**, the only one who can write a legend back to freedom.
- **Guide: Folio** — a sarcastic living book; tutorial voice and story narrator.
- **The Unwritten** — people born in the Margin with no story of their own. Our fully original champions (our IP); they are writing their story as the game goes.

## Story spine
Scribe wakes in the Margin → Folio explains the redaction → each Volume: infiltrate the Redactor's strongholds room by room (fits CMB-3), free its Revised legends, defeat its bosses → the Redactor's Final Draft (endgame, open-ended for live ops).
- Freeing a legend in story = champion reward (fits OVR-5, CMP-7).
- Campaign order, light → dark (D-18): Seedbed → Thousand Nights → Book of Kings → Nocturne → Final Draft.

## Tone & art
- Storybook dark fantasy: danger and gothic mood, but witty (Holmes and Scheherazade trading barbs).
- 2D side view; UI of ink, parchment and bookbinding; each Volume has its own palette.

## IP rules (all content)
- Use the **original text's** description, never a film/game version (no Universal Dracula/Frankenstein look, no Disney designs).
- Avoid trademarked characters (Zorro, Tarzan, Conan, John Carter, Popeye, Peter Pan).
- No religious figures. Respectful portrayal of every source culture.
- Lawyer check of final roster + game title before launch.
