## Rules
- **STY-1** Realm order (D-18): Prologue → **Seedbed** → **Thousand Nights** → **Book of Kings** → **Nocturne** → **Final Draft** (endgame, open for live ops).
- **STY-2** **Light → dark** (D-18): each realm is darker than the one before; the closer to the Redactor, the darker the world. Inside a realm too: ch1 open/bright land → last chapter the villain's dark stronghold.
- **STY-3** **5 chapters per legend realm** (D-20). Each chapter = one stronghold made of several stages (each stage 1–3 stars, CMP-2; stages per chapter Open), ends with a rescue and/or a boss.
- **STY-4** Legends are rescued, never fought (D-14): the Scribe reaches the ink-bound legend (final stage objective, CMB-6) and writes their name back with the Last Quill.
- **STY-5** Each legend's redaction twists their own tale (what they lost is what the Redactor took). Freeing restores it (D-23).
- **STY-6** Champions come mainly from summoning (OVR-5). Per realm, **2 rescues give the champion for free** (ch1 + ch5); other rescued legends join the Unbound in story and are obtained by **Recall** (summon). Count = config value, may change later (D-19).
- **STY-7** Teaching (CMB-32) runs once (D-26): Prologue = basics · Seedbed = party, room options, Herald · Thousand Nights ch1 Bleed-through, ch2 Margin, ch3 Gutter, ch4 hazards + props. Later realms mix, no re-teaching.
